The sea goes out a long way here and leaves the whole drowned country breathing. Look: the mud is full. A fiddler crab waves its one gold arm. The mudskipper crosses the water on its hands. The sea stars open under the glass of the shallows; carpet anemones hold the light like green mouths. The worms write and unwrite their wet script. I came with nothing to build and nothing to sell. I kneel and put my hand in the warm mud, and it gives, the way it always gave, and closes over my fingers, and holds.

[center]
tide back in over
the gold arm, the walking fish,
my hand. I drive home.
